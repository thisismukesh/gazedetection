# Visitor notice (print and place near the piece)

**Look Away** uses a camera to notice which way people are facing and looking.

- The camera detects head and eye direction only.
- Everything is processed on this computer, in real time.
- Nothing is recorded, stored or sent anywhere. No images, no face data.
- Faces are not identified, and nothing is inferred about age, gender or emotion.
